Colony Power – Electricity Billing App for Colonies

Colony Power is a free web app for colonies and shared electricity meters in Nepal. The administrator records meter readings, bills are calculated automatically for each Nepali (BS) month, payments and dues are tracked, and every consumer can log in to see their own bill.

  • Monthly meter readings and automatic bill calculation
  • Rates by Nepali (Bikram Sambat) month and year, with optional fixed monthly charge
  • Payments, dues and advance tracking for every consumer
  • Main meter comparison, notes and photos
  • English and Nepali, light and dark mode, installable on your phone

Built by Sohan Kumar Yadav, Janakpurdham-14, Nepal.
